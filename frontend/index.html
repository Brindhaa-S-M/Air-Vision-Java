Model methodology

A Random Forest regression model estimates PM2.5 using geographic coordinates, NO₂, aerosol optical depth, temperature, humidity, wind speed, and month.

Evaluation

MAE and RMSE summarize prediction errors, while R² describes explained variation on the reported evaluation split. These metrics are based on synthetic demonstration data.

Limitations

This academic prototype does not provide live monitoring, official AQI, health guidance, or validated future forecasts. Results should not be used for real-world decisions.
