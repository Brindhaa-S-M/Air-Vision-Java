Methodology and project limitations

Machine learning workflow

AirVision AI uses a Random Forest regression model to estimate PM2.5 from latitude, longitude, NO₂, aerosol optical depth, temperature, humidity, wind speed, and month.

MAE, RMSE, and R² are loaded from the backend's evaluation metrics. Training and test row counts describe the dataset split reported by that backend.

Responsible interpretation

The supplied training data is synthetic demonstration data. Strong metrics on synthetic data do not establish accuracy on actual environmental measurements.

This application is an academic prototype, not a live air-quality monitoring service, official AQI calculator, medical resource, or validated forecasting system.
